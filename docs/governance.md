# Project Governance

## Overview

The **k8s-agent-stack** project is committed to being a community-driven, open-source project. This document outlines the governance model for the project, including decision-making processes and roles.

## Roles

### Users
Users are community members who use the software. They are encouraged to participate by providing feedback, reporting bugs, and suggesting features.

### Contributors
Contributors are community members who contribute code, documentation, or other improvements to the project. Anyone can become a contributor by submitting a Pull Request.

### Maintainers
Maintainers are contributors who have shown a long-term commitment to the project and have been granted write access to the repository. Maintainers are responsible for:
- Reviewing and merging Pull Requests.
- Managing issues and project roadmap.
- Ensuring the project follows its goals and values.

## Decision Making

Decisions are made through a consensus-based model. Major changes should be discussed in GitHub Issues or Pull Requests before implementation.

### Technical Decisions
Technical decisions are primarily made by the Maintainers, with input from the community. We strive for technical excellence and adherence to open standards (CNCF, Linux Foundation).

### Conflict Resolution
In case of disagreement, the Project Lead has the final say. However, we aim to resolve conflicts through open discussion and consensus.

## Open Source Standards

We are committed to following open source best practices:
- **Transparency**: All development happens in the open on GitHub.
- **Inclusivity**: We welcome contributors from all backgrounds.
- **Interoperability**: We prioritize support for open protocols (A2A, MCP) and CNCF projects.

## Becoming a Maintainer

Contributors who consistently provide high-quality contributions and demonstrate leadership within the community may be invited to become Maintainers.
